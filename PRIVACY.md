# Sift privacy notes

- Barcode history is saved in the app’s protected Application Support directory on the iPhone.
- Sift sends a barcode to Open Food Facts or Open Beauty Facts only after the user starts a lookup.
- Sift does not create an account, collect a name or email, or use Supabase.
- Camera frames are used for barcode detection and are not recorded or uploaded by Sift.
- Ingredient scoring runs on the device after the ingredient text is returned or pasted by the user.
- Users can delete all local scan history from Settings.

The production privacy policy and the data practices of the third-party product databases should be reviewed before App Store publication.
